## 🛠️ Frequently Encountered Topics

### 1. Telegram Account SpamBot Restrictions
If your Telegram account has been restricted from sending direct messages to unknown users, this is an automated server-side measure by Telegram's global anti-spam algorithms.
- **Solution:** Open Telegram and search for the official verified bot: `@SpamBot`. Tap "Start" to check the reason and submit an appeal directly to Telegram's moderation team.

### 3. Account Deletion & Local Data Erasure
- **Local Data:** Simply log out in NayaGram (`Settings > Log Out`) or uninstall the app. All local cryptographic keys, session secrets, and cached messages are wiped instantly from the Android sandbox.
- **Telegram Account Self-Destruct:** You can configure automatic account deletion in `Settings > Privacy and Security > If Away For`.
